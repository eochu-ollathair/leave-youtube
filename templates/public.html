Choose subjects such as phones, politics, AI or fashion. Choose how many recent, highly watched videos to read for each subject. Add specific creators you want to hear from and block creators you do not trust or enjoy. Each morning, get a few concrete Telegram lines about what those videos said.

How it works

  • Finds recent videos and checks their view counts, dates and creators.
  • Reads available YouTube captions and picks concrete points spoken by each creator.
  • With your own optional text model, combines repeated claims and differing opinions.
  • Shows the selected videos and lets you preview or send a report yourself.

The free version needs no AI key. It runs on your computer, quotes useful speech, and sends it through your own Telegram bot. With your own optional text model, you can also ask why a claim matters and give the report your own angle, including sceptical questions about missing facts or possible motives. A motive is never treated as proven without evidence. The maker's local AI is not shared. Your private settings page needs your own opening key. Video claims are not independently proven facts.
