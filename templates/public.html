Open source · daily YouTube summaries

Leave YouTube.
Lose the adverts.
Keep the info.

Choose subjects such as phones, politics, AI or fashion. Choose how many recent, highly watched videos to read for each subject. Add specific creators you want to hear from and block creators you do not trust or enjoy. Each morning, get a few concrete Telegram lines about what those videos said.

How it works

  • Finds recent videos and checks their view counts, dates and creators.
  • Reads available YouTube captions and picks concrete points spoken by each creator.
  • With your own optional text model, combines repeated claims and differing opinions.
  • Shows the selected videos and lets you preview or send a report yourself.
  • Clears videos after sending them and never puts the same video in another report.
  • Lets you reply in Telegram to block a creator or add a new one.

The free version picks short quotes from video speech. To have it compare videos and explain why claims matter, you can connect your own AI. This website does not give other people access to the maker's AI. A suggested motive is only a possibility unless there is evidence.

Get your own copy

This is a computer program, not a phone app. It currently starts on Mac or Linux. It needs Python on that computer and your own Telegram bot. Keep the computer on for morning reports.
