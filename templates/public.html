Open source · daily YouTube summaries

Leave YouTube.
Lose the adverts.
Keep the info.

Choose subjects such as phones, politics, AI or fashion. Choose how many recent, highly watched videos to read for each subject. Block creators you do not trust or enjoy. Each morning, get a short Telegram report saying what those videos actually said.

How it works

  • Finds recent videos and checks their view counts, dates and creators.
  • Reads available speech text and combines repeated points across videos.
  • Names concrete claims and differing opinions instead of vague headlines.
  • Shows the selected videos and lets you preview or send a report yourself.

This is software you run for yourself. The private settings page needs your own opening key. Video claims are described as claims; they are not independently proven facts.
